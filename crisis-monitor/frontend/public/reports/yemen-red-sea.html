1 · Escalation

From shipping threat to a declared blockade

The Houthis moved from threats against Israel-linked shipping to a declared blockade aimed at Saudi interests, then to reported attacks on Saudi territory. Each step widened the set of states now watching the strait.

The sequence matters more than any single strike. A blockade that names Saudi-linked traffic turns a regional war into a shipping and energy problem for the Gulf, Europe and Asia.

2 · Bab el-Mandeb, the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden

The corridor and the coast it runs along

The strait links the Red Sea and the Suez route to the Indian Ocean. Traffic averaged about 280 transits a week in the reporting gathered here, close to the roughly 287 weekly figure reported for Suez. The period is not confirmed, so treat both as order-of-magnitude.

Hodeidah is the operational centre

Hodeidah, with Mocha to its south, handles the Houthi-held side of Red Sea trade. Israel struck the port in June 2025. Its current throughput could not be verified for this report.

The Gulf of Aden side is now Saudi-backed

Aden and Mukalla sit with the Saudi-backed government side. Traffic from the Gulf of Aden runs east toward Socotra and the Arabian Sea, where the new coalition would operate.

4 · Displacement

Over 200,000 people, with no series yet

The UN reported more than 200,000 people displaced by the escalation as of 7 October 2026. The figure is cumulative and was the only displacement number gathered here, so no trend line is drawn.

What would show a trend: monthly UN or IOM figures by governorate, and any movement toward Djibouti or Somaliland appearing in regional returns.

5 · Interactions with Horn groups

The Red Sea conflict is reaching the Horn

Al-Shabaab

AFRICOM flagged concern on 9 October 2026 about possible contact between Houthi networks and al-Shabaab. Evidence of operational cooperation is contested. Treat it as a risk, not a confirmed link.

Puntland and Somaliland

Bosaso on the Gulf of Aden coast and Berbera are both tied to Gulf and Israeli interest. Somalia's federal government has condemned Houthi threats against Saudi Arabia. Claims about Somaliland's recognition should be checked against current government statements.
